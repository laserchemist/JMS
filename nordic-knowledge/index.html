Abstract

Students increasingly take their course questions to private chatbots. The answers are not grounded in the course, classmates never see them, and instructors never learn what the class found confusing. Nordic Knowledge turns each question into a shared class artifact. Answers are generated by Claude and grounded in the instructor’s own uploaded materials (notebooks, slides, syllabus, data). Threads land on a course discussion board that AI helps organize, summarize and moderate, with the instructor in charge. Any thread can spawn a short quiz the whole class takes, and the aggregated results feed an instructor dashboard that shows engagement, topic trends, and the specific misconceptions behind wrong answers. Points, badges and a leaderboard reward asking, discussing and refining.

Why does my bootstrap confidence interval get narrower when I use 10,000 resamples instead of 1,000?

1 Good instinct to test it. It actually shouldn’t get much narrower. More resamples make the estimate of the interval’s edges less noisy. The width is set by your sample size (n = 40 in Lab 6) and the spread of the data.

# Lab 6 pattern: resample the sample, collect means
means = make_array()
for i in np.arange(10000):
    resample = sample.sample()
    means = np.append(means, np.mean(resample.column('bp')))
percentile(2.5, means), percentile(97.5, means)

Run it with 1,000 and 10,000 and compare the two intervals. Try a larger sample to see what really narrows it.

AI moderation

What “AI-moderated” means here

Organize In the app now

  • Ground every course answer in uploaded materials
  • Shape answers with per-course instructions
  • Classify threads into topic categories
  • Summarize each thread in a line or two
  • Filter & sort by time window and engagement

Moderate Next

  • Detect duplicates and suggest merging into one thread
  • Flag for review: off-topic posts or requests for graded-lab solutions
  • Surface unresolved threads with low ratings or no follow-up
  • Weekly digest to the instructor: what the class is confused about
  • Nudge students toward the relevant reading before answering
AI suggests, the instructor decides.Flags go to a review queue. Nothing is hidden or deleted without an instructor action.

Design principles

How Nordic Knowledge is built

Real data, honest empty states

Production dashboards show only what is in the database. When there is nothing yet, the page says so.

Scoped by role

Instructors see their own courses and students. Admins see the platform. The boundary is enforced in every query.

Instructor in the loop

AI drafts, classifies, summarizes and flags. Instructors approve quizzes and decide on moderation.

Small, documented modules

Each feature lives in a named Python service with docstrings and its own routes.

discussion_service.py · survey_module.py

Resilient AI configuration

Model names live in environment variables with a fallback chain, so a deprecation never takes the app down.

Verify the API, not just the UI

Every new view is checked against the JSON its endpoint returns. Consistency checks run from the Debug tab.

/admin/db-check
